Connecting to a projector

  1. Plug in the projector (HDMI, or USB-C with an adapter) and turn it on.
  2. Open System Settings → Displays. The projector should appear next to your built-in screen. Not there? Unplug the cable, wait a few seconds, and plug it back in.
  3. Make it a separate screen, not a mirror. Click the projector, then set Use as to Extended display. If it says "Mirror", the audience would see your whole screen — score and all.
  4. Check System Settings → Desktop & Dock → "Displays have separate Spaces" is on (it is by default). Otherwise full screen on the projector can black out your laptop screen.
  5. Keep the Mac awake: plug in the power adapter, then in System Settings → Lock Screen set the "turn display off" options to Never and the screen saver to Never.
  6. Plug in the power adapter. Supertitle Cuer keeps the Mac and both screens awake by itself while you're in Show mode — no sleep settings to change.
  7. Silence pop-ups: turn on Do Not Disturb from Control Center (the switches icon in the menu bar).
  8. In Supertitle Cuer, click 📽 Projector. Drag the new window onto the projector screen, then press F (or double-click it) for full screen.
  9. In Supertitle Cuer, click 📽 Projector. It opens full screen on the projector by itself. If it lands on your own screen instead, choose View → Move Projector to Second Screen.
  10. Test: click back in this window, switch to Show, and press → — the title should appear on the projector.

  3. Not showing up? Open Settings → System → Display → Multiple displays and click Detect.

  6. Silence pop-ups: turn on Do not disturb (click the clock/notification area at the bottom right).

  9. Test: click back in this window, switch to Show, and press → — the title should appear on the projector.
If something looks wrong
• The projector shows your whole screen → it's mirroring/duplicating: go back to step 3 (Mac) or 2 (Windows).
• Titles are too big or small → ⚙︎ Settings → Title size, and set Preview shape to match the projector.
• The projector window got closed → click 📽 Projector again; it reopens showing the current title.
• The projector opened on your own screen → View → Move Projector to Second Screen (it also moves there by itself when a projector is plugged in).
 • Arrow keys don't advance → click once anywhere in this main window to give it the keyboard.
